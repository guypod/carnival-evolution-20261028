# Balloon Darts

Nine balloons hang in a 3x3 rack. You have nine darts to pop them.

## How to play

- Click or press Space or Enter to start the round.
- Click a balloon to throw at it, or press 1 to 9 for the rack's cells: 1 is
  top left, 3 is top right and 9 is bottom right.
- A click that is not on a balloon is a miss.
- One dart flies at a time. A throw while a dart is in the air is ignored and
  costs nothing.
- When a link or button on the page has focus, the game leaves your keys alone.

## Scoring

- An ordinary balloon scores 20.
- The star balloon in the middle (key 5) scores 50.
- Pops in a row earn a streak bonus on top of the balloon's points: the first
  pop adds nothing, the second 5, the third 10, and the fourth and every one
  after it 15. A popping balloon shows its total and the bonus inside it.
- A popped balloon leaves a torn scrap and its knot in the balloon's colour,
  and the star's cell keeps its star.
- Each balloon pops once. A dart at a popped balloon, or a miss, uses up the
  dart, scores nothing and ends the streak, so the next pop starts again with
  no bonus.
- The bottom of the screen shows your score, your current streak, your best
  streak this round and the darts left.
- The round ends when the ninth dart lands. The results show your score, hits,
  accuracy (hits out of darts thrown) and best streak, and the score goes to
  the carnival's score board once. Click or press Space or Enter to play again;
  the new round starts with no streak.

The best possible round is 315: eight balloons and the star (210) with nine
pops in a row (0 + 5 + 10 + 6 x 15 = 105 in bonuses).
