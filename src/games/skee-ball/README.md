# Skee Ball

Roll ten balls up a sloped wooden alley into the scoring rings.

## How to play

1. Press **Start** (click or tap it, or press Space/Enter).
2. Pick one of three lanes: click or tap it, or use Left/Right. Picking a lane
   never rolls the ball.
3. Watch the power meter on the right sweep up and down. The gold band on the
   meter shows the power that reaches the chosen lane's centre ring.
4. Press the **Roll** button at the bottom right (click or tap it), or Space or
   Enter, to roll. Each press rolls one ball. You see the ball roll up the
   chosen lane, away from you, as far as the power you released it at, then
   rest in the ring it scored (that ring lights up) or short of the rings. You can't change lanes or roll again while
   it rolls.
5. After ten balls the round ends. Press **Play again** (click or tap it, or
   press Space/Enter) for a new round.

You can play a whole round with only a mouse or a touch screen.

Each lane's rings sit at a different height on the ramp, so each lane needs a
different power. Nothing is random: the same lane and power always score the
same.

## Scoring

Points depend on how far the ball lands from the chosen lane's ring centre,
measured as a share of the ramp's length:

| Distance from centre | Points |
| -------------------- | ------ |
| up to 0.04           | 50     |
| up to 0.09           | 20     |
| up to 0.15           | 10     |
| further              | 0      |

The screen shows balls left, your total and your last roll. Under the last
roll it says whether that ball had too little power (it stopped short of the
centre ring), too much power (it rolled past it) or was right on target (it
reached the centre ring), so you can learn each lane's power. Play again clears
it. Your total is saved
to the carnival's scoreboard once, when the tenth ball lands. If saving fails,
the result screen says so, and the game tries again once when you play again or
leave the booth.
