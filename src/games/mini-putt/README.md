# Mini Putt

Putt round a three-hole course of walls and bumpers in as few strokes as you can.

## How to play

1. Press **Start** (click or tap it, or press Space/Enter).
2. Aim: click or tap anywhere on the green to point the dashed aim line at that
   spot, or turn it with Left/Right.
3. Set the power: click or tap the **Power** meter below the green, or use
   Up/Down. The meter and the length of the aim line show the power, from 10%
   to 100%.
4. Press **Putt** (click or tap it, or press Space/Enter). The ball rolls,
   slows with friction and bounces off the edges, the wooden walls and the
   red bumpers. You can't aim or putt again until it stops.

   The red bumpers are the red discs with a cream centre, labelled **Bumper**
   on the green (three on hole 2, one on hole 3). They are obstacles, not
   targets or hazards: the ball bounces off them like off a wall, and hitting
   one costs no stroke and scores nothing. The start card says the same.

5. The ball drops into the cup only when it is over the cup and moving slowly
   enough. A fast ball rolls straight over.
6. When the hole ends, press **Next** (or Space/Enter) for the next hole. After
   the third hole you see your total; press **Play again** for a new round.

Each hole allows 6 strokes. If the ball is not in the cup when the sixth putt
stops, the hole ends unsunk. Nothing is random: the same aim and power always
roll the same way.

## Scoring

| Hole result       | Points                    |
| ----------------- | ------------------------- |
| Sunk in n strokes | 10 × (7 − n), so 60 to 10 |
| Not sunk in 6     | 0                         |

The screen shows the hole, strokes on this hole, total strokes and the score so
far. The final score, the sum of the three holes (0 to 180), is saved once, as
soon as the third hole ends. If it can't be saved, the result screen says so,
and it is tried again when you press Play again or leave the booth. If Play
again can't save it either, the result screen stays up so the score is not lost.
